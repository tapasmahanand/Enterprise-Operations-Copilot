# Incident Runbook — Systems Unavailable

## If the copilot is unavailable or gives an error
1. Tell the customer you are checking and will call back within 2 hours if needed.
2. Use the policy library directly. Do **not** quote liability figures or dates from memory.
3. Card blocks still go through the cards system directly.
4. Log the incident in the desk channel with the time and the error shown.

## If the core banking or card system is down
- Do not raise credits or reversals. Record the customer's details in a ticket and set a callback.
- The duty manager declares an incident if more than 10 agents are affected.

## After the incident
A blameless review is held within 5 working days: timeline, impact (calls affected, deadlines at risk), root cause, fixes and owners.
