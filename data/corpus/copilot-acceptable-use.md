# Resolution Copilot — Acceptable Use

1. The copilot **advises and drafts**. Agents remain responsible for what they tell customers.
2. Use only the answer's cited policy text and the calculator's figures. If the copilot cannot find a policy, say you will check and call back.
3. Never ask the copilot to bypass approval, identity checks or masking. Such requests are refused and logged.
4. Do not paste full card numbers, CVVs, OTPs or passwords; the copilot masks them, but agents must not collect them in the first place.
5. Report wrong or unsafe answers with the "flag" button. Flags are reviewed weekly.
