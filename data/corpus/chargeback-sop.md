# Card Chargeback Initiation

A chargeback asks the merchant's bank, through the card network, to return funds for a disputed card transaction.

## When to use it
- Goods or services not received, and the merchant does not respond within 15 days of the customer's complaint to them.
- Goods significantly not as described.
- Merchant refund promised but not received after 30 days.
- Do **not** use a chargeback for unauthorised transactions; those follow the liability policy (KB-POL-LIAB-A or KB-POL-LIAB-B).

## Evidence required before initiation
- Transaction id, merchant name, amount and date (from the transaction record).
- The customer's written description.
- Proof of contact with the merchant (email or chat), where available.

## Process
1. The copilot drafts the chargeback with a reason code: `not_received`, `not_as_described` or `refund_not_processed`.
2. Every chargeback initiation needs supervisor approval (KB-MATRIX).
3. After approval, disputes operations files it with the network. Network timelines are typically 30 to 45 days; never quote an outcome date to the customer.
