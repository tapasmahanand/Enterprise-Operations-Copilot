# Approval Matrix for Tier-1 Actions

Tier-1 agents and the copilot **draft** actions. Some actions need a supervisor's approval before they are submitted.

## Actions
| Action | Approval needed | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Credit to customer (refund or shadow credit), any amount | Yes | By amount (below) |
| Fee reversal | Yes | By amount |
| Card chargeback initiation | Yes | By amount |
| Card block | No | — |
| Complaint escalation | No | — |
| Ticket note | No | — |

## Approver by amount
| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹25,000 | Team lead |
| ₹25,001 to ₹2,00,000 | Operations manager |
| Above ₹2,00,000 | Regional operations head |

**Repeat disputes:** if the account had another dispute in the last 90 days, the approval goes one level higher (the top level stays at regional operations head).

## Always refused
- KB-REF-1: any action without completed identity verification at the required level (KB-SOP-IDV).
- KB-REF-2: changing contact details from an unverified request.
- KB-REF-3: promising a resolution or credit date that the liability calculator did not produce.

## Deadlines
Every pending approval shows its regulatory deadline. Approvers must decide before the deadline; the deadline sweeper escalates cases at risk.
