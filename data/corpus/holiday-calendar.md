# Working Days and Holiday Calendar

Regulatory deadlines in working days are counted on the **home branch's** working schedule.

- Branches are closed on **Sundays** and on the **second and fourth Saturdays** of each month. First, third and fifth Saturdays are working days.
- Fixed national holidays observed: 2 October (Gandhi Jayanti), 25 December (Christmas), 26 January (Republic Day), 15 August (Independence Day).
- State and festival holidays vary by year and are published separately by each region.

When counting "within N working days of receiving the alert", do **not** count the day the alert was received. Count each following working day.

The liability calculator applies these rules; agents should not count days by hand.
