# Fee Schedule and Fee Reversal Policy

## Common retail fees (illustrative, inclusive of GST)
| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| ATM withdrawal at another bank's ATM beyond the free limit | ₹23 per transaction |
| Foreign currency card transaction markup | 3.5% of transaction value |
| Debit card annual fee (classic) | ₹236 |
| Card replacement | ₹236 |
| SMS alerts | Free |
| Cheque return (insufficient funds) | ₹590 |

## When a fee can be reversed
- The fee was charged in error (for example, a free-limit ATM withdrawal charged as paid).
- The fee arose from a bank-side failure (for example, a declined transaction that was still charged).
- Goodwill reversal for a long-standing customer, at most once in 12 months per fee type.

## Process
1. Identify the fee on the statement and its reason code.
2. The copilot drafts a **fee reversal** with the amount from the statement.
3. Every fee reversal needs supervisor approval (KB-MATRIX), with the approver tier by amount.
4. Do not promise a reversal before approval.
