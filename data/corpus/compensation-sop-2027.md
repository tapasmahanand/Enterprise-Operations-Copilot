# One-time Compensation for Fraud Losses

Applies to fraudulent electronic transactions on or after 1 January 2027 where the customer is liable because of their own negligence (KB-POL-LIAB-B section 3).

## Eligibility
- Individual customer, including a sole proprietor.
- Gross loss up to ₹50,000.
- Reported to Kestrel **and** to the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in **within 5 calendar days** of the transaction.
- The customer has never received this compensation before (once per lifetime).

## Amount
The lower of 85% of the net loss and ₹25,000. Pay within 5 calendar days of the customer's application. The liability calculator works out eligibility and the amount.

Like every credit, the payment needs supervisor approval.
