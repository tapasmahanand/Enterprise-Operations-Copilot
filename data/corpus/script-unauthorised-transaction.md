# Call Script — Unrecognised Transaction

1. **Reassure:** "I'm sorry this happened. I'll block your card right now so no further payments can go through."
2. **Block** the card (KB-SOP-BLOCK), then confirm: "Your card ending XXXX is now blocked."
3. **Collect facts:** date and amount of each transaction, when the customer received the SMS alert, whether they shared any OTP or card details, whether they have reported it to the cybercrime helpline 1930.
4. **Explain liability using only the calculator's result.** For example: "Because you reported this within the allowed time, you won't be liable for this amount. We'll credit ₹X to your account by [date from the calculator] while we investigate."
5. **Approval:** "I've sent the credit to my supervisor for approval. You'll get an SMS confirming it within 24 hours."
6. **Close:** give the ticket number and suggest reporting to 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in if they haven't.

Never say "you will definitely get your money back" before approval.
