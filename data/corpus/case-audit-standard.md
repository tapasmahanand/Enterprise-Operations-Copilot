# Case Documentation and Audit Trail Standard

Every dispute or unauthorised-transaction case must record:
1. The customer's report date and time, and the date the bank's alert was received.
2. The policy and rule identifiers applied, and the liability calculation.
3. Every action drafted, who approved or rejected it, when, and why.
4. Every customer communication.
5. For any automated assistance, a trace of what the system retrieved and decided.

A case is **audit-complete** only when items 1 to 5 are all present. Compliance samples closed cases every month; incomplete cases are a finding against the desk.
