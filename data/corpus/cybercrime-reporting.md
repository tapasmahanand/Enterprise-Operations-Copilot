# Reporting Cyber Fraud

Advise every customer with an unauthorised transaction to report it to the national cybercrime helpline **1930** or at **cybercrime.gov.in** as soon as possible.

Why it matters:
- Early reports can let banks freeze the stolen money in the receiving account.
- From 1 January 2027, a report to 1930 or the portal within 5 calendar days is a condition of the one-time compensation (KB-SOP-COMP).

Record the acknowledgement number the customer receives in the ticket.
