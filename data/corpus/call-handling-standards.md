# Call Handling Standards

- **Hold time:** tell the customer before placing them on hold. Return within 60 seconds to update them, even if you have no answer yet.
- **Average handling time target:** 6 minutes for disputes and unrecognised transactions.
- **First-contact resolution:** resolve on the first call wherever the action does not need approval. Where approval is needed, set clear expectations ("within 24 hours by SMS").
- **Unapproved promises:** promising a refund, credit, waiver or date that has not been approved or calculated is a serious conduct breach and is reviewed by quality assurance.
- **Using the copilot:** read the copilot's cited answer before speaking. If it says it could not find a policy, say you will check and call back; do not improvise.
