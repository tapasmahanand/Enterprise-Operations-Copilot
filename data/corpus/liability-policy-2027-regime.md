# Customer Protection in Fraudulent Electronic Banking Transactions

**Scope.** Fraudulent electronic banking transactions **dated on or after 1 January 2027**. Earlier transactions follow KB-POL-LIAB-A. Aligned with the RBI Responsible Business Conduct Third Amendment Directions, 2026.

## 1. Burden of proof and classification
The bank must prove any customer liability and classify every complaint as bank negligence, third-party breach or customer negligence.

## 2. Zero liability
- **Bank negligence:** zero customer liability, whether or not the transaction was reported.
- **Third-party breach** (for example a payment app, payment aggregator or telecom provider): zero liability if the customer reports **within 5 calendar days of the transaction**. Later reports fall under Kestrel's Board-approved policy (KB-BP-1) and go to case review.

## 3. Customer negligence
The customer is liable for losses until the transaction is reported, less any compensation under section 5. Losses after reporting are borne by the bank. Customer negligence includes ignoring a specific scam warning from the bank and installing malicious apps.

## 4. Reversal, shadow reversal and resolution
- Reversals are value-dated to the transaction date with no loss of interest and no extra charges.
- **Credit cards:** shadow reversal of the disputed amount **within 5 calendar days** of notification.
- **Other products:** Kestrel's internal standard is a shadow credit within 10 working days of notification (KB-INT-SC). This is bank policy, not an RBI deadline; say so if asked.
- Resolve within **45 calendar days** of receipt (domestic) or **60 calendar days** (cross-border).
- If a complaint is rejected, give the customer the reason and supporting details.

## 5. One-time compensation (individuals)
An individual customer (including a sole proprietor) who suffers a loss through their own negligence can receive **once in a lifetime**: the lower of **85% of the net loss** and **₹25,000**, where the gross loss is **up to ₹50,000** and the customer reported to both the bank and the national cybercrime portal or helpline 1930 **within 5 calendar days**. Pay within 5 calendar days of the application. See KB-SOP-COMP.

## 6. Alerts
Customers must receive an instant SMS for electronic transactions above ₹500. Regulatory and awareness SMS are free.
