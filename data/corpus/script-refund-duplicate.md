# Call Script — Refunds and Duplicate Charges

**Merchant refund not received:** "Merchant refunds usually reach your account within 5 to 7 working days after the merchant processes them. Do you have the refund reference number from the merchant?" Then follow KB-POL-REFUND.

**Charged twice:** "I can see two payments of ₹X to [merchant] on [date]." If confirmed (KB-POL-DUP): "I've raised a request to credit the duplicate amount. It needs my supervisor's approval, and you'll get an SMS within 24 hours."

Do not promise the credit itself, and do not quote an amount the customer gave you; use the amounts on the statement.
