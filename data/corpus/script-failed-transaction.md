# Call Script — Failed Transaction

1. "Let me check that transaction for you." Look it up and confirm it shows as failed and debited.
2. Give the reversal date from KB-POL-TAT for that channel: "This is reversed automatically by [date]."
3. If the date has passed: "The reversal is late. You'll also receive ₹100 for each day of delay, credited automatically."
4. Add a ticket note.
5. If the customer is unhappy, offer escalation (KB-SOP-ESC).
