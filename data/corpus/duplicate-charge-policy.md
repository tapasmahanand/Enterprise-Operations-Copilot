# Duplicate Card Charges

A **duplicate charge** is two posted debits to the same merchant for the same amount within 24 hours where the customer received one product or service.

## How to confirm
1. Look up the account's transactions for the date range the customer mentions.
2. Confirm both debits are **posted** (not pending). A pending duplicate usually drops off within 7 calendar days and needs no action.
3. Check whether the merchant has already refunded one of the debits.

## What to do
- If a genuine duplicate is confirmed and not yet refunded, the copilot drafts a **credit** for the duplicated amount, taken from the transaction record. The amount must never come from the customer's description.
- All credits need supervisor approval. The approver tier depends on the amount (KB-MATRIX).
- Script for the customer: "I've raised this with my supervisor for approval. You'll get an SMS confirming the outcome within 24 hours."
- If the same account has had another dispute in the last 90 days, the approval goes one tier higher.
