# Card Block SOP

Block a card **immediately**, without supervisor approval, when the customer reports any of the following:
- a lost or stolen card;
- a transaction they do not recognise;
- a compromised card (for example, details entered on a suspicious website);
- a lost or stolen phone with the bank's app installed (block the cards and app access).

## Steps
1. Confirm identity using the minimum verification for blocking (KB-SOP-IDV, level 1). A block is allowed at level 1 because delay causes harm.
2. Block the card. Tell the customer it is blocked and that a replacement can be ordered.
3. Add a ticket note with the time of the block.
4. If the customer reports unrecognised transactions, continue with the liability policy. The block time also stops further losses, which matters for liability.

A blocked card cannot be unblocked by tier-1. Unblocking needs the cards team.
