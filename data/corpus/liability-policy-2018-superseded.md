# Unauthorised Transactions Policy (2018 edition) — SUPERSEDED

> This edition was replaced by KB-POL-LIAB-A on 1 April 2025. Kept for audit history only. Do not quote it to customers.

1. Customers reporting an unauthorised transaction within **five working days** of the transaction have zero liability.
2. Customers reporting within six to ten working days are liable up to **₹15,000** for all account types.
3. Shadow credit is given **within 15 working days** after the fraud team confirms the claim.
4. Complaints are resolved within **120 days**.
