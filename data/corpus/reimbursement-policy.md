# Customer Reimbursement for Bank-side Errors

When a customer loses money because of a Kestrel error (for example a wrong debit, a duplicate auto-debit or a standing instruction run twice), Kestrel reimburses the amount **and** any charges caused by the error (such as a cheque-return fee triggered by the wrong debit).

- The reimbursement amount comes from the transaction and fee records.
- It is a credit, so it needs supervisor approval (KB-MATRIX).
- Interest lost because of the error is added at the account's interest rate for the days affected.
- Out-of-pocket costs outside the bank (for example a late fee charged by a merchant) need a document from the customer and go to the operations manager regardless of amount.
