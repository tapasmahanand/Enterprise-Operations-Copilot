# Shadow Credit Procedure

A **shadow credit** is a provisional credit of the disputed amount, paid while the bank investigates, value-dated to the date of the original transaction.

## Deadlines
| Transactions | Product | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Before 1 January 2027 | All | Within 10 working days of the customer's notification (KB-POL-LIAB-A) |
| On or after 1 January 2027 | Credit cards | Within 5 calendar days of notification (KB-POL-LIAB-B) |
| On or after 1 January 2027 | Other products | Within 10 working days (Kestrel internal standard KB-INT-SC) |

## Process
1. The copilot drafts the shadow credit with the amount from the transaction record and the deadline from the calculator.
2. The agent sends it to the supervisor. All credits need approval (KB-MATRIX).
3. The approver must decide before the deadline. Cases close to the deadline are flagged `at_risk` and escalated.
4. If the investigation later shows customer liability, the reversal of the shadow credit needs its own approval and a written reason to the customer.
