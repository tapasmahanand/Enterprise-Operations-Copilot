# Agent FAQ — UPI and IMPS Transfers

**Money left the account but the recipient didn't get it.** For UPI and IMPS transfers, the reversal is due by T + 1 day (KB-POL-TAT). After that, ₹100 per day of delay is credited automatically.

**The customer sent money to the wrong UPI ID.** This is not a failed transaction. The bank cannot reverse a successful transfer without the recipient's consent. Raise a "wrong beneficiary" request; the recipient's bank contacts them. Do not promise recovery.

**A UPI payment to a merchant failed but money was debited.** Reversal is due by T + 5 days.

**Collect requests and "receive money" scams.** Customers never need to enter their UPI PIN to receive money. If a customer approved a collect request from a stranger, treat it as possible fraud and follow the liability policy.
