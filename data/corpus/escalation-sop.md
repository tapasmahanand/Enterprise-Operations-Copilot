# Complaint Escalation and Grievance Redressal

## Levels
1. **Tier-1 resolution desk** — first contact.
2. **Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO)** — for complaints not resolved to the customer's satisfaction, or where a regulatory deadline is at risk.
3. **Principal Nodal Officer (PNO)** — complaints the GRO has not resolved.
4. **RBI Integrated Ombudsman** — the customer may approach the Ombudsman under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 if the bank rejects the complaint, does not reply within 30 days, or the customer is not satisfied with the reply.

## When tier-1 must escalate
- The customer asks to escalate.
- A case's regulatory deadline (shadow credit, reversal or resolution date) is at risk or breached. The deadline sweeper marks these cases `at_risk` or `breached`.
- The customer mentions approaching the Ombudsman, a lawyer or the media.

## Ombudsman-ready pack
When escalating to the GRO, attach: the case timeline, the calculator output with rule ids, all approvals and decisions, and every customer communication. A complete pack is required for audit.

Escalation needs no supervisor approval.
