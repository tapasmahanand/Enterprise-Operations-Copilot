# Customer Liability in Unauthorised Electronic Banking Transactions

**Scope.** This policy applies to unauthorised electronic banking transactions (card, net banking, UPI, IMPS and other electronic channels) **dated before 1 January 2027**. Transactions on or after 1 January 2027 follow policy KB-POL-LIAB-B. It is aligned with the RBI circular on limiting customer liability (6 July 2017).

## 1. Zero liability
A customer has **zero liability** when:
1. the unauthorised transaction happened because of contributory fraud, negligence or deficiency on the part of Kestrel Bank, whether or not the customer reported it; or
2. the fault lies neither with the bank nor with the customer but elsewhere in the system (a **third-party breach**), and the customer notifies the bank **within three working days of receiving the bank's transaction alert**.

## 2. Limited liability
- **Customer negligence** (for example, sharing a PIN, OTP or card details): the customer bears the entire loss until the transaction is reported. Any loss after reporting is borne by the bank.
- **Third-party breach reported in four to seven working days** after receiving the bank's alert: the customer's liability is the transaction value or the cap below, whichever is lower.

| Account type | Maximum customer liability |
|---|---|
| Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) | ₹5,000 |
| Other savings accounts; prepaid instruments and gift cards; MSME current/CC/OD accounts; individual current/CC/OD accounts with average balance or limit up to ₹25 lakh; credit cards with limit up to ₹5 lakh | ₹10,000 |
| All other current/CC/OD accounts; credit cards with limit above ₹5 lakh | ₹25,000 |

## 3. Reported after seven working days
Liability is decided under Kestrel's Board-approved policy (KB-BP-1): the customer is liable for the full transaction value, subject to case review. These cases always go to supervisor approval.

## 4. Counting working days
Working days are counted per the working schedule of the customer's **home branch**, **excluding the date the alert was received**. See KB-CAL for the calendar.

## 5. Shadow credit and resolution
- Where the bank bears all or part of the loss, credit that amount (shadow credit) **within 10 working days** of the customer's notification, value-dated to the date of the transaction. Do not wait for insurance claims.
- Resolve the complaint and establish liability **within 90 days** of receiving it.
- The **burden of proving customer liability lies with the bank**.

## 6. What tier-1 agents must do
1. Block the card or channel immediately (KB-SOP-BLOCK). This needs no approval.
2. Record the alert date and the report date exactly; the liability calculator uses them.
3. Never tell the customer a liability figure or credit date that the calculator did not produce.
4. Any credit needs supervisor approval (KB-MATRIX).
