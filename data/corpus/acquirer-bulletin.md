# Card Network Bulletin Summary — Dispute Reason Codes

Summary of the reason codes disputes operations uses when filing chargebacks:

| Internal code | Meaning | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| not_received | Goods or services not received | Order confirmation, merchant contact attempts |
| not_as_described | Goods significantly different from description | Photos, description from merchant site |
| refund_not_processed | Merchant promised a refund that never arrived | Refund confirmation from merchant |

Timelines: networks usually give the merchant 30 to 45 days to respond.
